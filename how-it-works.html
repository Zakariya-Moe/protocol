01 · Serum concentration

How we model serum curves

When you inject a peptide or GLP-1 agonist subcutaneously, it doesn't hit your bloodstream all at once. It absorbs gradually, peaks, and then your body clears it over time. The shape of that curve — how fast it rises, how high it peaks, how long it lingers — is what we call the serum concentration curve.

Helio uses a standard one-compartment pharmacokinetic model with first-order absorption and elimination. This is the same foundational model used in clinical pharmacology research for subcutaneous peptide administration.

The formula

One-compartment PK model · Subcutaneous administration
C(t) = C_max × ( e−k_e×t − e−k_a×t )

Where:
C(t) = serum concentration at time t
C_max = peak concentration (dose-dependent)
k_a = absorption rate constant (1 / absorption half-life)
k_e = elimination rate constant (0.693 / elimination half-life)
t = hours since injection
Half-life values are sourced from published pharmacokinetic studies for each compound and listed in the compound library below. Bioavailability adjustments for subcutaneous administration are applied where clinical data is available.

Worked example — Tirzepatide 5mg

Example · Tirzepatide 5mg subcutaneous injection
Dose5 mg
Elimination half-life (t½)~116 hours (published Lilly data)
Elimination rate (k_e)0.693 / 116h = 0.00598 h⁻¹
Absorption half-life~24 hours (subcutaneous)
Time to peak (T_max)~48–72 hours post-injection
Trough level (day 7, pre-dose)~60% of peak remaining

This is why weekly dosing works for tirzepatide — the compound hasn't fully cleared before the next injection, so steady-state builds over several weeks. Helio's serum curve shows you exactly where you are on that curve right now.

The one-compartment subcutaneous model is well-established in the literature for GLP-1 receptor agonists. Tirzepatide PK parameters are sourced from the published Phase 3 SURPASS trial pharmacokinetic sub-studies.

Reference: Eli Lilly SURPASS-1 PK sub-analysis; Bergman et al., 2023
02 · Reconstitution calculator

The reconstitution math, explained

Lyophilized peptides like BPC-157, TB-500, and CJC-1295 come as powder that must be reconstituted with bacteriostatic water before injection. Getting the math wrong means dosing inaccurately — which for a compound costing hundreds of dollars per vial is both wasteful and potentially ineffective.

The formula

Reconstitution dose calculation
Draw volume (units) =
  ( desired_dose_mcg ÷ vial_total_mcg ) × bac_water_mL × 100

Note: 1mL = 100 units on a U-100 insulin syringe
Helio supports U-100 syringes (standard insulin syringe). If you use a different syringe type, contact your prescribing physician for dosing guidance.

Worked example — BPC-157 250mcg dose

Example · BPC-157 5mg vial, 2mL BAC water, 250mcg desired dose
Vial size5mg (5,000 mcg)
BAC water added2 mL
Concentration2,500 mcg / mL
Desired dose250 mcg
Draw volume10 units on a U-100 syringe
Doses per vial20 doses

Helio shows this as a highlighted fill line on a rendered syringe — you see exactly where to draw to, not just a number. The app also tracks remaining doses and alerts you when supply is running low.

03 · Muscle preservation

Why muscle tracking is the whole point

GLP-1 medications suppress appetite profoundly. In clinical studies, users in caloric deficit without structured resistance training lose a significant portion of weight as lean muscle — not fat. This "skinny fat" outcome is the most common failure mode of GLP-1 therapy for people who care about body composition.

In trials of semaglutide without exercise intervention, lean body mass loss represented approximately 25–39% of total weight lost. With structured resistance training, this figure drops significantly.

Reference: Wilding et al., STEP 1 trial body composition sub-analysis; Bianco et al., 2022

Helio tracks the three variables that determine whether you're losing fat or muscle:

↗

Log strength workouts. Helio tracks sessions per week and correlates training frequency with body composition trends. Three sessions per week is the clinical minimum for muscle retention during GLP-1 therapy.

Body composition inputs
Log weight and optionally body fat percentage from a smart scale or DEXA scan. Helio separates fat mass from lean mass trajectory so you can see whether you're losing the right kind of weight.

05 · Vial lifecycle

Every vial has a clock running.

Reconstituting a peptide vial is only half the equation. Once reconstituted, a vial has a finite viable window — typically 3–6 months refrigerated — and environmental deviations can shorten that dramatically. Most users have no idea when their vial expires, or whether it's still good after a power outage or a forgotten cooler bag.

Helio tracks the full lifecycle of every vial: when you opened it, when you reconstituted it, how many doses remain, and when it needs to be discarded. No more guessing.

Storage temperature rules

07 · FAQ

Questions we actually get

Is this medical advice?

No. Helio is an educational tracking tool. The serum curves and reconstitution calculations are based on published pharmacokinetic data and are provided for informational purposes only. Always work with a licensed physician for your protocol design, dosing, and any changes to your treatment plan.

How accurate are the serum curves?

The curves represent population-average pharmacokinetics from published studies. Individual variation can be significant — your actual peak, trough, and clearance will differ based on body composition, injection site, technique, and individual metabolism. Use the curves as directional guidance, not a precise measurement.

Can I use this for compounded GLP-1s?

Yes. The PK parameters for semaglutide and tirzepatide apply regardless of whether the compound is branded or compounded, since the active molecule is identical. Reconstitution calculations apply specifically to lyophilized compounded formulations — pre-mixed branded pens do not require reconstitution.

Why isn't my compound in the library?

We only include compounds with published PK data we can cite. If reliable half-life data doesn't exist in peer-reviewed literature, we won't add a number we can't verify. You can still add a custom compound with a manual half-life value — it just won't carry our verification badge.

Will this work alongside my clinic's prescription?

Yes — Helio is designed to complement your clinic relationship, not replace it. Log the protocol your physician prescribed, track your adherence, and export the data to share at your next appointment. The clinic portal (Phase 2) will allow your physician to view your logs directly with your permission.
